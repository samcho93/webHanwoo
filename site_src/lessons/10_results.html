4. Interpreting the results and limitations

  • Final ID count vs. actual herd size: the closer the post-stitching ID count is to the number of animals in the pen (about 11–13), the better. Leftover extra IDs are mostly ① animals in the neighboring pen briefly visible at the edge of the frame, and ② animals that were occluded for a long time and reappeared far away.
  • For the long video (cow03, 22 minutes), two-stage stitching alone still left 29 IDs, but after ReID + fixed-herd assignment (Lesson 08) the count is held at the estimated herd size N.
  • Absolute distance is an approximation based on body height and may be off by ±20–30%. However, relative comparisons between animals and between time periods within the same camera are reliable.
  • If ground-truth ID labels are available, you can evaluate quantitatively with standard tracking metrics such as MOTA, IDF1 and HOTA (TrackEval library).
